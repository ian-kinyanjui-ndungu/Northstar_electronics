Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about orders, shipping, returns, and more.

Orders

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also track your order by logging in to your account and visiting the My Orders page. Tracking updates are provided in real time by the carrier.

Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement, provided they have not yet been processed for fulfilment. To cancel, go to My Orders in your account dashboard and select Cancel Order. If the cancellation window has passed, you can initiate a return once the item arrives.

Order modifications (such as changing the shipping address or adding items) are possible within the first 30 minutes of placing an order. After that, the order enters our fulfilment pipeline and cannot be changed. Contact support as soon as possible if you need to make an urgent change.

Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping takes 2–3 business days. Overnight shipping delivers the next business day. Orders placed before 2pm PST are typically shipped the same day.

Yes! Standard shipping is free on all orders over $99. For orders under $99, a flat rate of $5.99 applies. Express and Overnight shipping options carry separate fees. See our Shipping Information page for full details.

Currently we ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and select EU countries. International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient. Delivery times for international orders vary by destination (typically 7–14 business days).

Returns & Refunds

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be in their original, unused condition with all original packaging and accessories included. Defective or incorrectly shipped items are eligible for free return shipping. See our full Returns & Refund Policy for details.

Log in to your account, navigate to My Orders, find the relevant order, and click Request Return. Follow the on-screen instructions to select items and a return reason. You'll receive a prepaid shipping label by email within 24 hours (for eligible returns).

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. The credit will then appear on your original payment method within an additional 3–7 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

Products

All products sold on Northstar come with the manufacturer's standard warranty. Warranty periods vary by product and brand. Proof of purchase from Northstar is required for warranty claims. To initiate a claim, contact us and we'll coordinate with the manufacturer on your behalf.

Each product page lists compatibility information in the specifications section. If you're unsure whether a product works with your existing setup, you can also contact our support team — we're happy to advise before you buy.

Account

Click Sign Up in the top navigation bar. Enter your name, email address, and a password, then verify your email. Once verified, your account is active and you can start shopping, tracking orders, and managing your preferences immediately.

We take security seriously. All data is transmitted over 256-bit SSL encryption. Passwords are hashed and salted — never stored in plain text. We are PCI-DSS compliant and never store your CVV. For full details, read our Privacy Policy.
