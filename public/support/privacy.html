Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Northstar Technologies ("Northstar", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it. It applies to all users of the Northstar website and platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Order information: Billing address, shipping address, and purchase history.
  • Payment information: Card type and last four digits (full card details are handled by our PCI-compliant payment processor and are never stored on our servers).
  • Device & usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time on site — collected automatically via cookies and server logs.
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders, including shipping and delivery communications.
  • Managing your account and providing customer support.
  • Sending transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates, returns).
  • Sending marketing emails and promotions where you have provided consent (you may opt out at any time).
  • Improving the platform through analytics and usage insights.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share data only with:

  • Shipping carriers — to facilitate delivery of your orders.
  • Payment processors — to securely process transactions (Stripe, PayPal, etc.).
  • Analytics providers — aggregated, anonymised data used to improve the platform.
  • Legal & regulatory authorities — when required by law or to protect our rights.

Any third-party service providers are contractually bound to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or alteration. All data in transit is encrypted using 256-bit TLS/SSL. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never accessible in plain text. We regularly review our security practices and conduct periodic security audits. Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to perform their job functions. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights or freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority as required by applicable law.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies on the Northstar platform. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us:

  • Essential cookies: Keep you logged in and maintain your shopping cart between sessions.
  • Analytics cookies: Understand how visitors interact with the site so we can improve it.
  • Marketing cookies: Deliver relevant advertising on third-party platforms (only with your consent).

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the platform.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data.
  • Right to portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw marketing consent at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@northstar.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK, or the relevant EU DPA).

7. Contact Information

For any privacy-related questions, concerns, or requests, please contact our Privacy Team at privacy@northstar.com or by writing to us at Northstar Technologies, 123 Innovation Drive, San Francisco, CA 94105, United States. We take all privacy enquiries seriously and aim to respond within 5 business days.
