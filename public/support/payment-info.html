Billing Information

Your billing address must match the address associated with your payment method. If there is a mismatch, your payment may be declined as a fraud-prevention measure. You can update your billing address at any time in your account settings. All transactions are in US dollars (USD).

Order Total Breakdown

Your final order total is calculated at checkout and includes:

  • Subtotal — combined price of all items in your cart
  • Shipping — calculated based on your selected shipping method and delivery address
  • Tax — applicable sales tax based on your shipping address
  • Discount / Promo Code — applied before tax and shipping where applicable

No additional charges will ever appear on your statement beyond the amount shown at checkout confirmation.
