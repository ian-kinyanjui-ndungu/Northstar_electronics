Shipping Information

Everything you need to know about delivery options, timelines, and costs.

⚡ Same-Day Dispatch: Orders placed before 2pm PST / 11pm EAT on business days are processed and shipped the same day.
🕐 Time Zone Reference: All order cut-off and processing times are listed in both PST (Pacific Standard Time, UTC−8) and EAT (East Africa Time, UTC+3). The difference is 11 hours — e.g. 2pm PST = 11pm EAT.

Processing Times

Most orders are processed within 1 business day. During peak periods (holidays, major sales), processing may take up to 2 business days. You will receive an email with tracking information once your order has shipped. Processing times do not count toward estimated delivery windows.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an automated email containing your tracking number and a link to track your package directly with the carrier. You can also view tracking information at any time by logging in to your account and visiting My Orders.

International Shipping

We currently ship to the following regions:

  • United States (all 50 states including Alaska & Hawaii)
  • Canada
  • United Kingdom
  • Select European Union countries
  • Africa — Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, and more (see full list at checkout)

International shipments may be subject to customs duties, taxes, and import fees. These charges are the sole responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our shipping fees.

Address Requirements

To ensure successful delivery, please double-check your shipping address before placing your order. We are unable to redirect parcels once they have been dispatched. Please note:

  • We ship to residential and commercial addresses.
  • We do not currently ship to PO Boxes for Express or Overnight orders.
  • A valid phone number is recommended in case the carrier needs to contact you.
  • Address changes can only be made within 30 minutes of order placement.
