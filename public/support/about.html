Our Story

Northstar started in 2019 in a San Francisco apartment with a simple premise: buying tech online shouldn't be a guessing game. Our founders — a group of engineers and designers who were constantly frustrated by confusing specs, dodgy sellers, and non-existent customer service — decided to build something better.

From a curated catalogue of 12 products, we've grown to over 500 SKUs across computing, audio, peripherals, networking, and smart home. Today, more than 50,000 customers trust Northstar as their go-to source for technology they can rely on. We're still the same team at heart — obsessive about product quality, relentless about customer experience.
