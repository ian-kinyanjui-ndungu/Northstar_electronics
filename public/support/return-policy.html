Returns & Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If something isn't right, we're here to make it easy.

📅
14-Day Return Window
Returns accepted within 14 days of the delivery date.
📦
Original Condition Required
Items must be unused, unaltered, and in original packaging.
🆓
Free Return Shipping
We cover return postage for defective or incorrectly shipped items.

How to Return an Item

  1. Log in to your account at northstar.com.
  2. Go to My Orders in your account dashboard.
  3. Click "Request Return" next to the order you'd like to return.
  4. Select items and reason — follow the on-screen prompts to choose items and a return reason.
  5. Ship the item back using the prepaid label emailed to you (eligible returns only), or your own postage.
  6. Receive your refund — once we receive and inspect the item, your refund is processed within 3–5 business days.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return or exchange:

  • Digital products, software licences, and download codes (once redeemed)
  • Items that have been opened, used, or show signs of wear
  • Items missing original packaging, accessories, or documentation
  • Customised or personalised products
  • Clearance or final-sale items (marked at time of purchase)
  • Items damaged due to misuse, accidents, or improper handling by the customer
